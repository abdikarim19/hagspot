About HagSpot

HagSpot is a web application created to help students at Augsburg University easily find and book study rooms, lab spaces, and meeting areas inside the Hagfors Center.

Our Purpose

Easy Booking

Students can quickly browse and reserve available spaces without confusion.

Real-Time Availability

See which rooms are open and avoid wasting time searching for space.

Better Organization

Helps manage shared spaces efficiently for both students and faculty.
